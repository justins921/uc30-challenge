Submitting An Offer Checklist

Goal: Submit offers that protect downside risk, preserve earnest money, maintain financing/inspection exits, and avoid emotional investing mistakes.

1. Property & Market Analysis

Fully analyzed the property with the CDS Rental Calculator and confirmed the deal meets my minimum buying criteria conservatively
Verified realistic market rents using multiple sources
Compared recently leased comparable properties
Confirmed conservative vacancy, maintenance, and CapEx assumptions
Stress tested lower rents and higher expenses

2. Financial Verification

Verified actual taxes and insurance
Reviewed leases and current rent roll
Confirmed utilities and owner-paid expenses
Reviewed financing terms and estimated payment
Confirmed total cash needed to close

3. Realtor & Lender Review

Realtor understands investment properties and cash flow
Lender reviewed financing structure
Property qualifies for intended financing
Reviewed seller motivation and market competition

4. Offer Protections

Included inspection contingency
Included financing contingency
Included adequate due diligence period
Earnest money protected during contingencies
Did NOT waive inspections or protections

5. Earnest Money & Due Diligence

Earnest money amount is reasonable
Earnest money is NOT released early
Due diligence allows inspections and contractor bids
Due diligence allows verification of leases, repairs, and expenses
Financing approval timeline is realistic

6. Final Decision Check

Offer is based on numbers — not emotion
I understand the downside risks
I am NOT forcing the deal to work
I am comfortable walking away if new information appears
I still want the property under conservative assumptions
Remember: Great investors protect downside risk first. Conservative analysis and strong contract protections create safer investments.
