> "The more agents who understand your buy box and believe you are a serious buyer, the more likely you are to receive great opportunities. *Training 2 (lines 759-789):*

**Proposed:** Remove the general statement from Training 1 (2 sentences). Training 2's "Multiplication Effect" section is more specific and impactful.

---

**Overlap 3: Explaining your buy box to realtors**

*Training 1 (lines 325-330):*
> "If possible, connect with a realtor you already know and trust. Explain your buy box and your real estate investing goals. Make sure they understand that if they bring you a property that meets your criteria, you are ready to buy."

*Training 2 (lines 649-659):*

**Proposed:** Keep both. Training 1 is action-oriented ("do this today"), Training 2 is strategic ("here's why it matters"). These serve different purposes despite similar language.
